// The challenge

From idea to market in four steps

  1. 01

    Build

    Registered students have about a month to build a product with AI.

  2. 02

    Launch

    The product goes live on YLand, where anyone can use it.

  3. 03

    Run

    It stays on the market for a set period while YLand records its verified users and verified revenue.

  4. 04

    Rank

    Projects are ranked under the official challenge rules.

Dates, prizes and the full rules haven't been announced yet. Register and we'll send them to you.

// The platform

About YLand

YLand is a store for software and games built around one idea: how a product really performs should be visible. Next to each product, YLand shows figures drawn from actual activity on the platform, such as revenue, orders and paying users, instead of relying on ratings and marketing copy.
