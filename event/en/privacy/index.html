Privacy notice

This notice is a draft and is being reviewed before the challenge opens.

Who we are

The YLand Australian Student AI Challenge is run by YLand, a company based in Hong Kong. This notice explains what we collect on this website and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you send a request, we collect the categories you pick and the description you write. We don't ask who you are.

When you register, we collect your name, email address, school or university, whether you are under 18, and your description of what you plan to build. If you are under 18, we also collect the name and email address of a parent or guardian.

For every submission we store a scrambled form of your IP address that can't be turned back into the address. We use it only to limit spam.

How we use it

We use registration details to run the challenge and to contact you, and your parent or guardian if you are under 18, about it.

We use descriptions to work out which kinds of software people want and which kinds students plan to build. The website shows category names and counts only. It never shows your description, name, email address or school.

AI processing

Each description is sent to an AI service operated by Anthropic, which sorts it into categories. This may involve processing outside Australia. We send the description text only, never your name, email address or school, so please don't put personal details in a description.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of your details, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them and withdraw from the challenge.

Contact

A contact address will be published here before the challenge opens.
