How revenue is shared

Creators are paid by this month's usage share

Subscription fees in YDA go into one revenue pool. Each month the platform counts every app's active users and pays creators by usage share.

  1. 1

    Usage share = an app's active users this month ÷ the sum across all participating apps

  2. 2

    Revenue share = equals the usage share

  3. 3

    YDA paid = this month's distributable pool × revenue share

What counts as active use

  • In one month, one subscriber counts at most once for the same app.
  • A subscriber who uses several apps counts once in each of them.
  • Opening or using the same app again does not add to its count.
  • Active use is triggered by a clear event, such as a successful feature call; the exact condition is still being defined.

The denominator is every app's users this month added up. A subscriber who uses several apps counts once in each, so it can be larger than the number of subscribers.

Whether payment fees or a platform share come out of the pool first is still to be confirmed.

What subscribers get

What you can use as a subscriber

Each creator decides what subscribers get in their own app. There are three kinds.

  • Full access

    Use every feature of the app.

  • Partial access

    Use the features or allowance the creator opens up, such as a monthly quota.

  • Trial

    A time-limited or usage-limited trial, such as 14 days or the first 3 chapters.

Every app's details page spells out its benefit, limits and any extra fees before you start.
