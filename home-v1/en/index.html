The YLand game rules

YLAND / YOUR LAND

Everyone raises the questions, and everyone decides. There are two kinds of vote.

Read the full rules

Type A

Raise a question · One person, one vote

Anyone taking part can raise a question. Everyone holds the same single vote, so A, B, C and D hold 25% each.

  • A25%
  • B25%
  • C25%
  • D25%

Type B

Solve a question · Weighted vote

Voting power is split 25% each across four roles: creators of the idea, investors who put money in, contributors who add value, and participants who believe in the new world.

  • Creator25%
  • Investor25%
  • Contributor25%
  • Participant25%

Example: how Type B weights add up

Voting weights after YLAND and four users, A, B, C and D, take part

What happened

  1. AA buys a YLand membership, gets API access and an investor vote.Investor
  2. AA launches an AI Trader, earns a 50% management fee on its returns and gets a contributor vote.Contributor
  3. BB uses the AI Trader, keeps 50% of the returns and gets a participant vote.Participant
  4. CC launches AI Translator, earns 200 YDA in subscriptions and gets a matching participant vote.Participant
  5. DD buys a YLand membership, gets API access and shares the investor vote with A, 12.5% each.Investor
  6. DD launches an AI social app, earns 800 YDA in subscriptions and gets a matching participant vote.Participant
  7. DD launches an AI Trader with a 20% management fee and shares the contributor vote with A (A 8%, D 17%).Contributor
